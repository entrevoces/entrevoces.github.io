¡Corre y se va! The caller draws. The room hunts.

Project this, or pass one device around. The caller card speaks in your study language — boards carry the English. Tap the matching cell on your board; a full row, column, or the whole board shouts ¡Lotería! Wrong taps sting (the room will hear about it, kindly). Made for a local translation class: no accounts, no setup, just voices.

Boards: 2–4 players choose below · Caller: button or Space · Demo mode (in your Hub profile) adds English subtitles under every called card, for guests who don't speak the language yet.
